Custom PCB Design

Two custom PCBs designed in KiCad: a breadboard power supply with multiple voltages and an H-bridge motor driver.

Tools KiCad
Deliverables Circuit Schematic PCB Layout Final PCB

Breadboard Voltage Regulator

For this project, I created a custom PCB capable of supplying 3 different voltages to my breadboard: 12V, 5V, and 3.3V. It receives 12V through a barrel jack, the screw terminal supplies 12V, the right rails supply 3.3V, and the left rails supply 5V. I first created the circuit on a breadboard, and then transferred the circuit into KiCad. I then organized the components for the PCB design and had it manufactured.

H-Bridge Motor Driver

I created a custom PCB capable of driving a DC motor in both directions. It is specifically designed to work with a microcontroller. It receives 12V through a barrel jack or through a screw terminal, and has an LED to indicate when power is available. Additionally, the motor current traces can handle 12V and 5A continuously without melting. To first test my circuit, I created an H-bridge on a breadboard. After that, I created the circuit schematic in KiCad and designed the PCB.
